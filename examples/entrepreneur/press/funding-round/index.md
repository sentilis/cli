# NexusOps Raises $2.5M Seed Round

We are thrilled to announce that NexusOps has closed a $2.5M seed funding round led by Frontier Ventures, with participation from several strategic angel investors from the DevOps and SRE communities.

![Team Celebration](attachments/celebration.png)

This milestone allows us to double down on our mission: making infrastructure management as easy as editing a markdown file. "The complexity of modern cloud stacks is the biggest bottleneck in tech today," says Jordan Lee, CEO of NexusOps. "We are building the 'easy button' for reliable scale."

## Roadmap Highlights

- **NexusCore Engine:** Our proprietary declarative automation engine.
- **Multi-Cloud Sync:** Native support for AWS, GCP, and Azure with zero-config migrations.
- **Predictive Scaling:** AI-driven resource management to reduce cloud waste.

Read more about our engineering vision in our [Technical Roadmap](roadmap.md).
